1. Introduction

Welcome to Mbagi. We are committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our website and services.

2. Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of Mbagi, including:

  • Restaurant and food business owners and employees
  • Orphanages and recipient organizations
  • Volunteers and delivery personnel
  • Website visitors and general users
3. What Data We Collect
3.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you register or use Mbagi services, we may collect:

  • Account information: full name, email address, phone number, residential address
  • For businesses: company name, tax ID (NPWP), business type, operating hours
  • For recipients: organization name, bank account details, number of beneficiaries
  • Food information: type, quantity, pickup time, allergen specifications
  • Payment data: (if applicable) payment method, transaction history
3.2 Information We Collect Automatically

When you access our platform, we automatically collect:

  • Device information: device type, operating system, browser version
  • Access logs: IP address, visit time, pages accessed, session duration
  • Location data: GPS coordinates (with your permission) for matching and delivery
  • Cookies and tracking: preferences, session security, usage analytics
  • Platform interactions: file uploads, button clicks, messages sent
4. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  1. Provide and improve Mbagi services (automated matching, delivery, notifications)
  2. Verify identity and protect against fraud
  3. Send important notices and product updates
  4. Analyze trends and user behavior for platform optimization
  5. Calculate social and environmental impact (CO₂ reduced, food saved)
  6. Send monthly impact reports and business care certificates
  7. Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations
  8. Conduct research and program evaluation for public benefit
5. How We Share Your Data

We only share your data in the following situations:

5.1 Sharing Required for Service Delivery

We share the minimum information necessary with:

  • Delivery partners/couriers: recipient name, address, food details, GPS coordinates
  • Payment providers: transaction details (without storing full card numbers)
  • Verification team: data for KYC process and fraud prevention
5.2 Sharing Based on Your Consent

If you provide written permission, we may share:

  • Impact data for public reports (without individual identity)
  • Success stories and testimonials for marketing campaigns
5.3 Sharing Due to Legal Requirements

We may disclose your data if required by law, including to:

  • Government agencies and law enforcement authorities
  • Ministry of Social Affairs, Agriculture, or Environment
  • Tax authorities (for beneficiary reporting)
5.4 Sharing We DO NOT Do
  • We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties
  • We do NOT share data for marketing purposes without explicit permission
  • We do NOT share credit card numbers or passwords
