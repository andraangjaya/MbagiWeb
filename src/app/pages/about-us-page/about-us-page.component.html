Our Story
]
Mbagi was established to tackle the challenge of food waste across Indonesia. Since its inception, the platform has grown to support a community of over 12,000 registered users dedicated to sustainable consumption. By bridging the gap between surplus and need, Mbagi has successfully redistributed 21,100+ meals, contributing to a significant 27% reduction in total food waste volume within its operational areas.
Mulai Berbagi
[ Our Mission ]

Our mission is to feed more and waste less, a goal we achieve by serving as the essential link between surplus resources and community needs. By connecting food businesses directly with orphanages and recipient organizations, we ensure that perfectly edible food serves a purpose rather than becoming waste. Through our platform, we utilize automated matching and a dedicated network of volunteers to bridge the gap between abundance and hunger across Indonesia.
